About Learnify

Smart Learning & School Management Platform

Learnify is a comprehensive educational management system designed to simplify communication between students, parents, tutors, and administrators. The platform helps manage student registrations, subjects, fees, reviews, and notifications in a secure and user-friendly way.

Our goal is to provide a transparent learning environment where parents can track their child’s progress, students can focus on learning, and tutors can manage their responsibilities efficiently.
